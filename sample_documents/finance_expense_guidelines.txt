
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT GUIDELINES
Finance Department - Version 2.1

ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
- Business travel (flights, hotels, ground transportation)
- Client entertainment (pre-approved)
- Professional memberships and subscriptions
- Office supplies and equipment under $500

SUBMISSION PROCESS
1. Submit expense reports within 30 days of purchase
2. Attach all receipts and documentation
3. Include business purpose for each expense
4. Route to manager for approval

TRAVEL POLICIES
- Book flights at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Hotel stays limited to company-approved rates
- Daily meal allowance: $75 domestic, $100 international

APPROVAL LIMITS
- Under $500: Direct manager
- $500-$5,000: Department head
- Over $5,000: CFO approval required

REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
Approved expenses reimbursed within 10 business days.
