
DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY POLICY
Effective Date: January 2024

PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines for handling sensitive data and maintaining security compliance across all departments.

SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with access to company systems or data.

DATA CLASSIFICATION
- Confidential: Financial records, personal information, trade secrets
- Internal: Business plans, internal communications, project documentation  
- Public: Marketing materials, published content, general company information

SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
1. All confidential data must be encrypted at rest and in transit
2. Multi-factor authentication required for all system access
3. Regular security training mandatory for all employees
4. Incident reporting required within 24 hours of discovery

COMPLIANCE
This policy ensures compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable data protection regulations.

VIOLATIONS
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
