
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - ACME CORPORATION
Last Updated: January 2024

SECTION 1: WELCOME AND COMPANY OVERVIEW
Welcome to ACME Corporation! We're excited to have you join our team. This handbook provides important information about our policies, procedures, and benefits.

SECTION 2: WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible work arrangements may be available with manager approval.

SECTION 3: TIME OFF POLICIES
- Vacation: Employees accrue 15 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Personal Days: 3 days per year
- Holidays: We observe 12 company holidays annually

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work agreements must be documented and reviewed quarterly.

SECTION 5: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
We encourage continuous learning and provide up to $2,000 annually for approved training, conferences, and educational programs.

SECTION 6: CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior, respect colleagues, and uphold company values of integrity, innovation, and inclusion.
